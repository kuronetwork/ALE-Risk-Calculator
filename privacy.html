Welcome to Kuro Risk Calculator ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. 2. Information We Collect

We prioritize your privacy and minimize data collection:

  • Usage Data: We may collect non-personal usage data (e.g., browser type, device type) to improve our application's performance and compatibility.

4. Data Storage and Security

Since the core functionality operates locally in your browser, your risk assessment data remains on your device regardless of whether you are online or offline. We implement appropriate technical measures to protect any data that might be incidentally collected.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.
